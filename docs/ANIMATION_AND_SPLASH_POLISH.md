# PennyPal Animation & Splash Polish

This pass focuses on presentation quality without changing the application's functional scope.

- Landing header is fixed while the landing page scrolls.
- The header keeps the PennyPal wordmark, Sign in, and Create account visible.
- Landing content uses staggered fade/slide reveals.
- The landing background has a subtle animated navy/blue/mint gradient glow.
- App route transitions use a refined left-to-right entrance with fade and slight scale.
- The Android native launch background no longer renders the oversized logo before Flutter's designed splash.
- The Flutter splash remains the branded animated splash and is the single intended splash experience.
- Bottom navigation remains intentionally stable rather than animating with every tab switch.

The design goal is polished motion, not constant movement: animations are short, subtle, and should not interfere with finance tasks or accessibility.
